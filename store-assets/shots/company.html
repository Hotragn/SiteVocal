Frequently asked questions

How fresh is the coffee?

Every bag is roasted on Monday and leaves our roastery by Wednesday, so it reaches you within a week of roasting.

Can I pause my subscription?

Yes. You can skip a month, change your roast or cancel from your account page at any time, with no fees.

Do you ship outside the US?

We currently ship to the United States and Canada.
